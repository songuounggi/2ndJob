Before you begin
How to use this planner
No right order, nothing to keep up with. Use the pages you need and skip the rest.
1
Start with a brain dump.Your head is for having ideas, not for storing them. Write everything down first. Sorting can come later, or never.
2
Pick three, not thirty.Choose one lead and two smaller things. Three finished beats ten half-done.
3
Make the first step tiny.“Write the report” won’t get you started. “Open the document” will.
4
Plan in buffers.Everything takes longer than it looks. Leave room between blocks.
5
Reward the effort.Fill in the dopamine menu on a good day, and use it on the hard ones.
